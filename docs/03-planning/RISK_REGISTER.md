## 1. Status Tag Taxonomy & Risk Governance

### 1.1 Evidence & Research Status Tags
- **`[V]` Verified Primary:** Verified directly from primary peer-reviewed literature, official government guidelines, or primary technical documentation.
- **`[V*]` Verified Secondary/Vendor:** Sourced from secondary summaries, law-firm analyses, or vendor documentation; must be confirmed from primary text before appearing on a slide.
- **`[CONFLICT]` Sources Disagree:** Research sources report conflicting figures or dates (e.g., Das et al. denominator $25,253$ vs. $28,020$; DPDP commencement 13 Nov 2025 vs. 14 Nov 2025); conservative handling rule is locked.
- **`[GAP]` Knowledge/Evidence Gap:** No reliable primary fact or legal precedent exists; the system parameterizes the value and makes zero unverified claims.
- **`[verify]` Pre-Event Verification Required:** Requires an empirical hardware/sandbox check from §8 of this register prior to final pitch.

### 1.2 Weakness & Risk Disposition Tags (Closure F15)
- **`CLOSED`:** Mitigated by a deterministic architectural mechanism and verified by a named automated test (`T-*` / `ST-*`). Even for `CLOSED` rows, the technical assumption or boundary condition is explicitly stated (never a bare `"none"`).
- **`BOUNDED`:** Residual risk cannot be zeroed by software alone, but is bounded within a stated quantitative or operational envelope and monitored by a named runtime alarm.
- **`OPEN`:** Legal, policy, or hardware-tier dependency requiring external action before any real-data deployment; assigned a named owner and closing evidence requirement.
